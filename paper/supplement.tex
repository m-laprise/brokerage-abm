\begin{center}
{\large\bfseries Supplementary Material}\\[2pt]
{\large Alternative measures of broker structural advantage}
\end{center}

The main results section measures the broker's structural advantage by betweenness
centrality. The figures below reproduce the same analyses with the broker's two
other saved ego-network measures, Burt's aggregate network constraint and Burt's
effective size, both recomputed for the broker every \pv{suppMeasInterval} periods.

\begin{figure}[htbp]
\centering
\includegraphics[width=\linewidth]{supp_S1_grid_lines.png}
\caption{Broker Burt constraint (left) and broker effective size (right) across the
$\rho\times\delta$ grid; one line per gain $\delta$, one marker per
regime, each a late-window mean over that regime's seeds. Whiskers are
95\% Monte Carlo intervals.}
\label{fig:supp-grid}
\end{figure}

\begin{figure}[htbp]
\centering
\includegraphics[width=\linewidth]{supp_S2_position.png}
\caption{Broker Burt constraint (top) and broker effective size (bottom). Left: the
measure over time at the baseline, as \pv{suppRollWin}-observation
rolling ensemble means over seeds, with pointwise 95\% Monte Carlo intervals,
measured every \pv{suppMeasInterval} periods.
Right: one dot per one-at-a-time regime, the measure against access
fraction, each a late-window mean over that regime's seeds. Time starts at
$t=\pv{suppAxisStart}$ (end of burn-in).}
\label{fig:supp-position}
\end{figure}

\begin{figure}[htbp]
\centering
\includegraphics[width=\linewidth]{supp_S3_advantage.png}
\caption{Rank-correlation gap (broker minus principals, top) and output gap (broker
channel minus self-search, bottom) against broker Burt constraint (left) and broker
effective size (right); one dot per regime, each a late-window mean over
that regime's seeds, colored by the matching parameter $\rho$.}
\label{fig:supp-advantage}
\end{figure}

\begin{figure}[htbp]
\centering
\includegraphics[width=0.9\linewidth]{supp_S4_network_dynamics.png}
\caption{Broker Burt constraint (left) and broker effective size (right) over time at
the baseline regime, as \pv{suppRollWin}-observation
rolling ensemble means over seeds, with pointwise 95\% Monte Carlo intervals,
measured every \pv{suppMeasInterval} periods.
Axes start at $t=\pv{suppAxisStart}$ (end of burn-in).}
\label{fig:supp-dynamics}
\end{figure}
