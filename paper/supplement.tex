\begin{center}
{\large\bfseries Supplementary Material}\\[2pt]
{\large Alternative Structural Measures}
\end{center}

The first three figures reproduce the main structural-advantage analyses with the
broker's two other saved ego-network measures, Burt's aggregate network constraint
and Burt's effective size. Both are recomputed for the broker every
\pv{suppMeasInterval} periods. As in the main text, a regime is one scientifically
distinct parameter configuration; duplicate grid coordinates and inactive
parameters do not create additional regimes.

\begin{figure}[htbp]
\centering
\includegraphics[width=\linewidth]{supp_S1_grid_lines.png}
\caption{Broker Burt constraint (left) and broker effective size (right) across the
$\rho\times\delta$ grid. Lines for each difficulty level $\delta$ span
$\rho=0$ through $0.85$. The pure-quality boundary $\rho=1$, where $\delta$ is
inactive, is shown once and unconnected. Markers are late-window means over seeds
and whiskers are 95\% Monte Carlo intervals.}
\label{fig:supp-grid}
\end{figure}

\begin{figure}[htbp]
\centering
\includegraphics[width=\linewidth]{supp_S2_position.png}
\caption{Broker Burt constraint (top) and broker effective size (bottom). Left: the
measure over time at the baseline, as \pv{suppRollWin}-observation
rolling ensemble means over seeds, with pointwise 95\% Monte Carlo intervals,
measured every \pv{suppMeasInterval} periods.
Right: one dot per one-at-a-time regime, the measure against access
fraction, each a late-window mean over that regime's seeds. Time starts at
$t=\pv{suppAxisStart}$ to omit the initialization transient.}
\label{fig:supp-position}
\end{figure}

\begin{figure}[htbp]
\centering
\includegraphics[width=\linewidth]{supp_S3_advantage.png}
\caption{Difference in holdout rank correlation (broker minus principal, top) and output gap (broker
channel minus self-search, bottom) against broker Burt constraint (left) and broker
effective size (right); one dot per regime, each a late-window mean over
that regime's seeds, colored by the matching parameter $\rho$.}
\label{fig:supp-advantage}
\end{figure}
