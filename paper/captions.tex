\begin{ptitle}{information}
Sources of the broker's ranking advantage
\end{ptitle}
\begin{pcaption}{information}
Ranking advantage is the broker-minus-principal difference in rank correlation between predicted and expected match output. The full pair model uses Ridge regression with both parties' characteristics and their interactions. Restrictions give the broker as many observations as a typical principal, retain only one party's characteristics, or omit pair interactions. A: each restriction minus the full pair model at baseline. Diamonds are mean differences paired by random seed; whiskers are 95\% Monte Carlo intervals over \pv{nBaselineSeeds} seeds. Negative values indicate a smaller ranking advantage. B: late-window ranking advantages across the \pv{ablationConditionN} regimes in the $\rho\times\delta$ design. Small points are regime means, lines connect the same regime across models, and diamonds mark medians across regimes. Estimates average periods 481--500 within each seed before averaging across seeds. Dashed lines mark zero.
\end{pcaption}
