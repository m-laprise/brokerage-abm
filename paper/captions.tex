\begin{ptitle}{position}
Broker centrality and access fraction
\end{ptitle}
\begin{pcaption}{position}
Left: broker betweenness centrality and access fraction over time at the
baseline, as \pv{rollWin}-observation rolling ensemble means over
seeds, with pointwise 95\% Monte Carlo intervals; centrality is measured every
\pv{betwInterval} periods. Right: all 20 combinations in the $\rho\times\eta$
grid, with other parameters held at baseline, each shown as a late-window mean
over that regime's seeds. Color identifies turnover $\eta$, marker shape
identifies $\rho$, and whiskers are 95\% Monte Carlo intervals on both axes.
Within each $\eta$, lines connect $\rho=0,0.5,0.85$. The pure-quality boundary
$\rho=1$, where complementarity is absent, is shown unconnected. Time-series
panels begin at $t=\pv{axisStart}$ to omit the
initialization transient.
\end{pcaption}

\begin{ptitle}{grid}
Outcomes across the matching problem ($\rho\times\delta$)
\end{ptitle}
\begin{pcaption}{grid}
Six outcomes across the $\rho\times\delta$ grid; one line per gain
$\delta$, one marker per regime, each a late-window mean over that regime's seeds.
Whiskers are 95\% Monte Carlo intervals.
The rank-correlation gap is broker minus principals; the output gap, broker
channel minus self-search.
\end{pcaption}
