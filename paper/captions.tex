\begin{ptitle}{position}
Broker betweenness centrality and access fraction
\end{ptitle}
\begin{pcaption}{position}
Top row: ensemble means over time at the baseline regime pair (no capture, blue;
capture, orange), as \pv{rollWin}-observation rolling means; betweenness is
measured every \pv{betwInterval} periods. Bottom: late means across the
one-at-a-time no-capture regimes, one dot per regime, colored by the late-mean
output gap (broker minus self). Axes start at $t=\pv{axisStart}$ (end of
burn-in).
\end{pcaption}

\begin{ptitle}{ranklines}
Late-window means vs. effective rank
\end{ptitle}
\begin{pcaption}{ranklines}
Four outcomes against the effective rank $r_{90}$ of the matching function;
late means, across the $\rho\times\delta$ grid ($\rho=1$ omitted) plus the
one-at-a-time $\rho$ regimes; one line per gain $\delta$, no capture.
\end{pcaption}

\begin{ptitle}{grid}
Late-window means across the matching-function grid
\end{ptitle}
\begin{pcaption}{grid}
Nine outcomes across the $\rho\times\delta$ grid, late means (no capture;
$\rho=1$ omitted; one-at-a-time $\rho$ regimes refine the $\delta=0.5$ line),
one line per gain $\delta$. The first column is on the unit scale; in columns
two and three, panels in the same row share a y-axis.
\end{pcaption}

\begin{ptitle}{advantage}
Structural measures vs. informational and output gaps
\end{ptitle}
\begin{pcaption}{advantage}
Rank-correlation gap (broker minus agent) and output gap (broker minus self)
against broker betweenness centrality and access fraction; late means, one dot
per no-capture regime. Color gives $\rho$; marker size gives the effective rank
$r_{90}$.
\end{pcaption}

\begin{ptitle}{capture}
Captured demand share across regimes
\end{ptitle}
\begin{pcaption}{capture}
Captured share of outsourced demand. Top row: late means (line) and $\pm 1$ SD
over seeds (band) along the $\rho$, $N$, $f_r$, and $\eta$ sweeps. Bottom row:
captured share against four late-mean outcomes, one dot per capture regime (all
\pv{nCapture}), colored by $f_r$.
\end{pcaption}

\begin{ptitle}{dynamics}
Baseline dynamics over time
\end{ptitle}
\begin{pcaption}{dynamics}
Without capture (top row) and with capture (bottom row): matches per agent,
agent network degree (mean, solid; median, dotted), broker betweenness
centrality, access fraction, and outsourcing rate. Lines are
\pv{rollWin}-observation rolling ensemble means; betweenness is rolled over its
\pv{betwInterval}-period measurements. In each panel, pale gray reproduces the
corresponding series of the other row, so the two regimes can be compared
directly. The capture row's last panel adds the capture share, the captured
share of total demand (dashed). Panels in the same column share y-ranges; axes
start at $t=\pv{axisStart}$.
\end{pcaption}
