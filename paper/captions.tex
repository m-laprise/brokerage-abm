\begin{pcaption}{position}
Broker betweenness centrality and access fraction. Top row: ensemble means over
time at the baseline regime pair (no capture, blue; capture, orange), as
\pv{rollWin}-observation rolling means; betweenness is measured every
\pv{betwInterval} periods. Bottom: late means across the one-at-a-time no-capture
regimes, one dot per regime, colored by the late-mean output gap (broker minus
self). Axes start at $t=\pv{axisStart}$ (end of burn-in).
\end{pcaption}

\begin{pcaption}{ranklines}
Late means of four outcomes against the effective rank $r_{90}$ of the matching
function, across the $\rho\times\delta$ grid ($\rho=1$ omitted) plus the
one-at-a-time $\rho$ regimes; one line per gain $\delta$, no capture.
\end{pcaption}

\begin{pcaption}{grid}
Late means of nine outcomes across the $\rho\times\delta$ grid (no capture;
$\rho=1$ omitted; one-at-a-time $\rho$ regimes refine the $\delta=0.5$ line), one
line per gain $\delta$. The first column is on the unit scale; in columns two and
three, panels in the same row share a y-axis.
\end{pcaption}

\begin{pcaption}{advantage}
Rank-correlation gap (broker minus agent) and output gap (broker minus self)
against broker betweenness and access fraction; late means, one dot per
no-capture regime. Color gives $\rho$; marker size gives the effective rank
$r_{90}$.
\end{pcaption}

\begin{pcaption}{capture}
Captured share of outsourced demand. Top row: late means (line) and $\pm 1$ SD
over seeds (band) along the $\rho$, $N$, $f_r$, and $\eta$ sweeps. Bottom row:
captured share against four late-mean outcomes, one dot per capture regime (all
\pv{nCapture}), colored by $f_r$.
\end{pcaption}

\begin{pcaption}{dynamics}
Baseline dynamics over time without capture (top row) and with capture (bottom
row): matches per agent, agent network degree (mean, solid; median, dotted),
broker betweenness centrality, access fraction, and outsourcing rate. Lines are
\pv{rollWin}-observation rolling ensemble means; betweenness is rolled over its
\pv{betwInterval}-period measurements. In each panel, pale gray reproduces the
corresponding series of the other row, so the two regimes can be compared
directly. The capture row's last panel adds the captured share of total demand
(dashed). Panels in the same column share y-ranges; axes start at
$t=\pv{axisStart}$.
\end{pcaption}
